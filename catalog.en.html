🌌 Cosmic horizons & shells (12)

The observable horizons, the Hubble & CMB scales, and the theoretical boundaries of the observable Universe. Sorted by increasing comoving distance.

Laniakea (~520 Mly)

Comoving distance: 520.0 Mly · z = ~0.12 · tLB = 1.6 Gyr · age then: 12.2 Gyr

Our home supercluster — the approximate limit of the cosmic “neighborhood”. It contains ~100,000 galaxies.

1 Gly

Comoving distance: 1.00 Gly · z = ~0.24 · tLB = 2.9 Gyr · age then: 10.9 Gyr

At this distance, cosmic structure is well formed. The Universe was ~3 billion years younger.

z = 0.5

Comoving distance: 6.17 Gly · z = 0.50 · tLB = 5.1 Gyr · age then: 8.7 Gyr

Half the age of the Universe. Galaxies were smaller and more active. The star-formation rate approaches its peak.

z = 1

Comoving distance: 11 Gly · z = 1.0 · tLB = 7.7 Gyr · age then: 6.1 Gyr

The peak of galactic activity — the most intense period of star formation in the history of the Universe.

★ Hubble sphere (v = c)

Comoving distance: 15 Gly · z = 1.46 · tLB = 9.8 Gyr · age then: 4.0 Gyr

THE HUBBLE LIMIT: Galaxies at this distance recede at exactly the speed of light. Photons emitted TODAY from these galaxies will NEVER reach us — although we can see their old light.

★ Event horizon

Comoving distance: 16 Gly · z = →∞ · tLB = — · age then: —

THE ABSOLUTE CAUSAL HORIZON: No signal emitted by us TODAY can reach galaxies lying beyond. The accelerating Universe permanently limits what we can influence.

z = 2 (quasars)

Comoving distance: 17 Gly · z = 2.0 · tLB = 10.4 Gyr · age then: 3.4 Gyr

The heyday of quasars — supermassive black holes at their most active. The Universe concentrated more mass in compact structures.

First galaxies z ≈ 6

★ CMB (z = 1100)

Comoving distance: 46 Gly · z = 1100 · tLB = 13.8 Gyr · age then: 380,000 years

SURFACE OF LAST SCATTERING: The most distant thing directly observable. The CMB photons have traveled 13.8 billion years. Temperature: 2.725 K. Comoving distance 45.6 Gly — the place the CMB was emitted from, computed at the present moment. The difference of ~0.9 Gly from the Particle Horizon represents the space that could expand between the Big Bang and Recombination. Its fluctuations generated all of today's cosmic structure.

★ Observable Universe

Comoving distance: 47 Gly · z = ~1100 · tLB = 13.8 Gyr · age then: Big Bang

THE PARTICLE HORIZON: The absolute theoretical limit — the maximum comoving distance from which any signal (even primordial gravitational waves) could have reached us since the Big Bang. Standard ΛCDM value (Planck 2018, H₀=67.4): ~46.5 Gly. Note: 45.6 Gly (CMB) ≠ 46.5 Gly (Horizon) — they are distinct quantities, both correct. The difference is the opaque interval between the Big Bang and Recombination.
